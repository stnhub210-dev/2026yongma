Eat Like a Local, 10 Minutes from Gangneung Station

Just arrived on the KTX and hungry? Skip the chains by the station and walk 600m to Ponam-dong's Yongma Street, a neighborhood food street that locals have used for decades. On Friday and Saturday evenings it turns into a night market.

Dishes to try

  • Chodang sundubu — soft tofu made with seawater, Gangneung's signature
  • Gamja ongsimi — chewy potato dumplings in broth
  • Gangneung jjamppong — spicy noodle soup loaded with East Sea seafood
  • Hangwa & Gangneung coffee — for dessert

Travel light

Drop your suitcase at the free luggage room on event days (Fri & Sat, 5–10 PM). Show ID, receive a numbered tag, and pick your bag up before closing.

On weekdays

The street festival runs only on Fridays and Saturdays, but most of the 168 shops on Yongma Street are everyday local businesses and open on weekdays too.

Walk Yongma Street in 3D

Tap the floor to move, drag to look around. Check the shops and signs before you visit.

Zone map

Food Belt (Eoseowa Zone), Play Zone, Busking Zone and Flea Market along Yongma Street. Luggage room at the street.
