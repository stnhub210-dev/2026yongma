Gangneung's Local Food, All on One Street

Gangneung's best-known dishes are spread across town — tofu in Chodang, coffee in Anmok. On Friday and Saturday evenings, the K-Food Belt zone at Yongma Food Street brings them together, 600m from KTX Gangneung Station.

FAQ

What is the K-Food Belt?

A Korean government program that highlights regional dishes. At Yongma Food Street, 10 participating shops serve Gangneung specialties.

Is it spicy?

Jjamppong is spicy; tofu and ongsimi are mild.

Are there vegetarian options?

Tofu dishes are a good start; ask at each booth about ingredients.
