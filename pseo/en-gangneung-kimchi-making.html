Make Your Own Kimchi in Gangneung

Want to do more than eat? At Yongma Food Street's Play Zone you can mix seasoning into salted cabbage and make Gangneung-style kimchi with your own hands. Foreign visitors receive an English certificate.

Schedule

The Play Zone runs 4 times during the season, 5–9 PM. Check exact dates on the official programs page before you go.

Also at the Play Zone

Korean calligraphy on fans, postcards and tote bags; a giant claw machine; and works by local Gangneung artists.

Getting there

600m from KTX Gangneung Station. Leave your luggage at the free luggage room (Fri & Sat, 5–10 PM).

Zone map

Food Belt (Eoseowa Zone), Play Zone, Busking Zone and Flea Market along Yongma Street. Luggage room at the street.

FAQ

Do I need to book?

You can apply through the 'Join' page of the official website.

Is there English guidance?

Yes. The foreign visitor booth offers multilingual guidance.

Can kids join?

The experience is designed for all ages and nationalities.
