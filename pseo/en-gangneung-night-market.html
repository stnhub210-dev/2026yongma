Gangneung's Friday & Saturday Night Market

Every Friday and Saturday evening this autumn, a 40-year-old neighborhood street in Ponam-dong becomes a car-free night market. Yongma Food Street (용마미식거리) is a 10-minute walk from KTX Gangneung Station, with local food, street music and a small flea market.

What to eat

The Eoseowa Zone, part of Korea's K-Food Belt program, brings Gangneung's local dishes into one street: Chodang tofu seasoned with seawater, gamja ongsimi (potato dumpling soup), spicy seafood Gangneung jjamppong, traditional hangwa sweets and Gangneung coffee. There are 16 food booths plus long-running local restaurants.

What else

Street performances run 5–7 PM, followed by a DJ music café and street karaoke from 7 PM. A flea market of local makers opens 7–10 PM. On Oct 30–31, a free K-culture concert takes place on a special stage at Children's Park No. 8, a short walk away.

FAQ

Is it open every day?

No. Only Fridays and Saturdays, 5–10 PM, from Oct 9 to Nov 14, 2026.

Can I leave my suitcase?

Yes. A staffed luggage room at the street stores bags for free on event days, 5–10 PM. ID is required. Valuables and electronics are not accepted.

Is there English information?

Use the language button on the official website for English, Chinese and Japanese. Foreign visitors can also join a kimchi-making experience and receive an English certificate.
